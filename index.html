Questions teams ask first

What can the AI agents do?

They place and track orders, follow up with departments and outside parties, track inventory, enter production data, and prepare purchase and sales invoices. Your team approves what matters.

How do you control metal loss and theft?

Every issue and return is weighed and logged against a job. Loss is tracked by worker, department, date and item code, and anything over the limit is flagged.

Can staff work from LINE or WhatsApp?

Yes. Staff and suppliers send updates and photos by LINE or WhatsApp, and the agent records them.

Which modules are included?

17 modules cover production, stock, sales and purchasing, finance, and people.
